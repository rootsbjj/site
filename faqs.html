Before your first class

Questions?
Good.

Every black belt asked them too.

I have no martial arts experience — can I still start?

Yes, of course! The classes are suitable for all levels, from complete novice through to black belt. Everyone on our mat started exactly where you are.

What should I bring to class?

For your first class, wear comfortable clothing and bring a bottle of water. A few mat rules: no clothes with pockets, remove all jewellery, tie back long hair, and no shoes on the mats.

Do I need a mouth-guard?

We encourage adults to wear mouth-guards to training. We recommend a boxing-style mouth-guard for your top teeth.

Can I do private classes instead of group classes?

Private classes are the best way to develop your techniques, and they’re suitable for students of all levels. Contact us to arrange a time with one of our coaches.

Do I have to compete?

No. You can progress through the entire BJJ grading system — white to black belt — without ever competing. Competition is there for those who want it.

Can kids do BJJ?

Yes, of course! ROOTS BJJ has an extensive training program for children and teens, with a curriculum designed to keep training safe and age-appropriate at every stage.

Can women do BJJ?

Definitely! BJJ is a great way to build strength and coordination, and to learn real self-defence. See our women’s program.

When is a good time to start?

Now. We train all year round — there are no terms. Contact us to arrange your free trial class any week of the year.

Do you have a privacy policy?

Yes, we have a privacy policy — and we never share or sell any of your private information to third-party providers.
