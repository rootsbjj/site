Before you ask

Totally normal
to be nervous.

I’ve never done any martial arts. Is that OK?

That’s exactly who this class is for. Every black belt in our academy started with zero experience — classes are built for complete beginners.

Am I fit enough to start?

Yes. You go at your own pace from day one — jiu-jitsu is how you get fit, not a fitness test to enter.

What do I need to bring?

Comfortable clothes and a water bottle. That’s it — no gi or equipment needed for your first class.

Is there a lock-in contract?

The trial is completely free with no obligation. If you love it — and you will — we’ll talk memberships after.
