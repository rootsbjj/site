Women in jiu-jitsu

Women have trained martial arts for centuries — a London poster from around 1910 already advertised “women only classes” in jiu-jitsu — but their place in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is more recent, with the sport officially recognised in 1970.

Yvone Duarte made history as the first female competitor in 1985 and the first woman ever awarded a black belt, in 1990. She fought for female inclusion, persuading the Rio de Janeiro federation to create a women’s division — the first competition was held in 1985.

Recently there has been a massive increase in girls and women training BJJ worldwide — and our mats are proof.
