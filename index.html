Our roots

Before Brazilian jiu-jitsu had a home in Australia, one Brazilian black belt brought it here.

ROOTS BJJ was the first Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu gym to be opened in Australia by a Brazilian black belt. Our vision is simple: empower people mentally and physically to live a better, more balanced life through the benefits of martial arts.

Discipline. Respect. Self-confidence. Self-defence. Focus. Mental strength. Coordination. Real training, real friendships — real jiu-jitsu, taught the way it was passed down to us.
